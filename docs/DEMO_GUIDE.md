# Epoch presentation guide

## Opening pitch

Creators know when viewers leave, but deciding what to edit still takes guesswork. Epoch connects possible retention problems to timestamped evidence from video, audio and scripts. It explains the concern, shows a counter-explanation and keeps the creator in control. The retention curve is an explicit scenario, not invented audience analytics.

## Five-minute demonstration

1. **Introduce the problem (30 seconds).** Show the hero and three cards: find the moment, understand the mechanism, approve the decision.
2. **Explain the three inputs (45 seconds).** Switch Video, Audio and Script in the pipeline infographic. Video supplies shots, frames and sound; audio supplies speech and delivery; scripts provide text with estimated timing. All converge on narrative analysis, rules and reviewable evidence. Missing modalities stay unknown.
3. **Show the hosted demo (60 seconds).** Play the supplied Hindi Short, select the trailer, and show the audio/script choices. Inspect Retention, Findings, Transcript and Jev routing. Explicitly identify all hosted analytical values as seeded examples. Use Start walkthrough for the complete 25-step explanation.
4. **Show real local analysis (two minutes).** Open a completed run. Overview connects survival, departure rate, transcript risk, cumulative watch time, sections and findings. Retention adds pressure relative to baseline, selected-moment attribution and priority windows. Survival cannot rise; changing pressure and slopes explain the undulations. Text shows word rate, new ideas, lexical question/answer relations, abstract passages, dependencies, rhythm and the transcript. Voice shows speaking rate, pitch, pitch spread and voiced share. Audio shows RMS level, LUFS and silent gaps. Outputs provides files, shots, sampled OCR and stage provenance. Shared timestamps connect each view to the source. Older scoring views are separately labelled. The 44-step media walkthrough explains each view; script reviews have 37 steps.
5. **Close on creator control (45 seconds).** Jev can keep, rewrite, shorten or request review. Low-confidence answers require review; validators constrain edits and protect announced replays. Accepted decisions become a plan. Edit externally and reanalyse to compare actual sources. Export packages preserve provenance.

## Questions from judges

- **Is retention measured?** No. Current curves are uncalibrated scenarios with explicit baseline anchors. Assumption sensitivity is not a statistical confidence interval.
- **What is measured?** Available source timing, waveform, loudness, shot/frame evidence and alignment are measurements or model extraction outputs. Interpretation remains reviewable.
- **Does the public site run AI?** No. It is a browser-only seeded showcase. The local application runs the actual pipeline.
- **Is Jev connected?** Yes, the stage and on-demand review route are integrated. Fresh Hindi runs returned low-confidence needs-review decisions, which demonstrate routing rather than editorial accuracy.
- **Why use completed runs?** Local ASR can be slow. The supplied trailer's fresh ASR took about 19.5 minutes. Use completed results during a live presentation.
- **What remains incomplete?** Visual AI remains unqualified; fresh Hindi runs lacked OCR and visual coverage. No audience-ground-truth calibration or broad language accuracy claim is established.
